## Project diary

After any meaningful change to the product direction, data model, spreadsheet/import workflow, public page structure, navigation, or editorial rules, append a dated entry to `PROJECT_DIARY.md`.

Each entry must record:

- what changed;
- why the user requested or approved it;
- its status (`Implemented`, `Deferred`, `Replaced`, or `Planned`);
- any important effect on content entry or website behaviour.

Preserve earlier entries when a decision changes. Add a new entry explaining the replacement instead of rewriting history. Do not add diary entries for trivial formatting or mechanical refactors unless they reflect a product decision.
